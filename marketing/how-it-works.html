The Goal Is Not to Automate Judgment.
The Goal Is to Make the Intake Path Clear.

A caller reaches the firm. The attorney may be in court, with a client, at home, or away from the desk.

The question is not whether software should decide what happens next.

It should not.

The question is whether the firm gets a usable intake record: who called, what happened, what practice area it appears to involve, whether a consultation was requested or booked, and whether the record needs faster review.

TrueVow is designed around that path.

Step 1 — Confirm the Intake Path

First, the Call Path Is Confirmed.

TrueVow does not begin by asking the firm to rebuild its systems. The first step is confirming how intake should work around the firm's current reality:

  • ✓ where calls route
  • ✓ which practice areas Benjamin should handle
  • ✓ which questions Benjamin should ask
  • ✓ when a caller should be escalated
  • ✓ how consultations should be requested or booked
  • ✓ which alerts the firm should receive
  • ✓ whether recordings or transcripts should be enabled
  • ✓ how records should be reviewed

Setup confirmed before activation

Benjamin should not go live until the firm has reviewed the intake path, the greeting, the legal-boundary language, the booking flow, and the attorney-review process.

Step 2 — Routed Call

Benjamin Answers Through the Selected Intake Path.

When a call is routed to Benjamin, Benjamin follows the firm's configured intake path. That means the caller is not dropped into a free-form chatbot. Benjamin asks selected questions, captures structured responses, follows booking rules, and flags records for review based on configured criteria.

The flow can support yes/no questions, short structured answers, and approved open-ended caller responses where appropriate.

Sample Benjamin opening

"Hi, I'm Benjamin. I help the firm collect basic intake information for attorney review. I cannot provide legal advice, and speaking with me does not mean the firm has agreed to represent you. I'll ask a few structured questions so the firm can review what happened."

Controlled intake, not legal advice

Benjamin does not provide legal advice, create an attorney-client relationship, run conflicts checks, accept or decline a matter, or replace attorney review.

Step 3 — Structured Record

The Call Becomes a Reviewable Intake Record.

The point of the call is not just to answer the phone. The point is to create a record the firm can actually review.

Depending on the selected intake path, Benjamin can capture:

  • ✓ caller name and contact details
  • ✓ practice area
  • ✓ incident location or jurisdiction signal
  • ✓ incident timing
  • ✓ injury or treatment signal
  • ✓ police report or documentation signal
  • ✓ adverse-party references
  • ✓ language preference
  • ✓ booking details
  • ✓ priority context
  • ✓ escalation flags

Step 5 — Booking + Alerts

The Firm Gets the Record, the Alert, and the Next Step.

Based on the firm's configuration, Benjamin can request or book consultations through the selected calendar and booking rules.

The firm can receive alerts with key details:

  • ✓ who called
  • ✓ what the intake appears to involve
  • ✓ whether the record is priority review
  • ✓ whether a consultation was requested or booked
  • ✓ what the firm should review next

If recordings or transcripts are enabled, those are handled according to the firm's policy, disclosure requirements, jurisdiction, and retention settings.

Clear Boundaries Matter.

Attorneys do not need vague automation near intake. They need to know exactly where the line is.

Benjamin does

  • Answer routed intake calls through the selected path
  • Ask selected PI intake questions
  • Capture structured intake records
  • Flag priority context based on configured criteria
  • Request or book consultations based on firm rules
  • Alert the firm for review

Benjamin does not

  • Provide legal advice
  • Create an attorney-client relationship
  • Run conflicts checks
  • Accept or decline a matter
  • Value a case
  • Make settlement or litigation decisions
  • Replace attorney review

You Do Not Need a Full Platform Migration to Start.

Some legal technology platforms begin with broad workflow change: documents, discovery, medical summaries, demand letters, case management, and full lifecycle automation.

TrueVow starts smaller and earlier. For solo and small PI firms, the first operational gap often appears before a case exists:

  • ✓ calls reach the firm
  • ✓ notes stay thin
  • ✓ callbacks happen late
  • ✓ intake details scatter
  • ✓ booking is inconsistent
  • ✓ priority is unclear
  • ✓ attorney review becomes harder than it should be

TrueVow starts with the call path because that is where the first reviewable record is either created clearly — or never properly formed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Benjamin an AI chatbot?

No. Benjamin is not a free-form legal-advice chatbot. Benjamin follows selected intake templates, routing rules, booking settings, escalation logic, and attorney-review boundaries. It can capture approved open-ended caller responses where appropriate, but it does not provide legal advice or make legal judgments.

Does Benjamin record calls?

Recording and transcript features are optional. If enabled, they are configured during setup based on firm policy, disclosure requirements, jurisdiction, and retention settings.

How does intake priority work?

Benjamin may apply priority context based on configured intake criteria. Priority labels help the firm decide what to review first. They do not decide whether a matter should be accepted, declined, valued, or pursued. Every intake record remains visible inside the portal. TrueVow INTAKE does not use public per-lead unlock fees.

Do I need to change my case-management system?

No. TrueVow starts with the front end of intake: routed calls, selected questions, booking, alerts, and structured intake records. No full case-management migration is required to begin.

What practice areas does Benjamin support?

TrueVow is currently positioned for solo and small plaintiff-side personal injury firms. Additional practice areas may be reviewed during setup, but the public product is optimized around PI intake, booking, priority alerts, and attorney-review workflows.

How long does setup take?

Setup timing depends on routing, calendar access, intake configuration, recording policy, and firm readiness. Before activation, the firm reviews the intake path, tests Benjamin, and confirms the workflow.

Do I have to use TRACE or SETTLE?

No. INTAKE works as the starting product. TRACE and SETTLE are later workflows and should be discussed only when the firm is ready for those stages.

What is the ROI?

ROI depends on call volume, consultation booking rate, average fee assumptions, and the firm's current intake process. The ROI calculator is directional only. It is not a promise, prediction, or guarantee.
