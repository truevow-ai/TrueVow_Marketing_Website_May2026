TrueVow TRACE — Client Engagement & Case Readiness

TRACE Remembers the Work. You Handle the Decisions.

TRACE is a deterministic pre-litigation operating layer for solo and small PI firms — from engagement and claim setup through treatment, records, evidence, documented damages, deadline review, and explainable readiness.

Not another practice management system. TRACE focuses on the operational work inside an active PI matter: what needs attention, what the firm is waiting on, what is missing, and what still blocks readiness.

Built for PI pre-litigation

TRACE sits between an approved prospect and SETTLE. It is designed to make the Matter operationally complete and evidentially ready while your existing PMS remains the firm’s general administrative system. Plans: Start $35 · Essential $179 · Complete $299 per activated Matter.

The Matter Is Active. The Operational Work Should Not Live in Memory.

Once representation is approved, pre-litigation work starts moving in parallel: claims, treatment, records, evidence, damages support, deadlines, and client follow-up. TRACE keeps those dependencies visible without forcing the firm into another general-purpose PMS.

The fragmented way

  • ✕ Carrier follow-up lives in email
  • ✕ Record requests live in spreadsheets
  • ✕ New providers surface late
  • ✕ Missing bills and wage proof are found near demand
  • ✕ Deadline review depends on memory
  • ✕ Nobody can explain exactly why the file is not ready

With TRACE

  • ✓ Claim and coverage state is visible
  • ✓ Providers, requests, productions, and follow-ups are linked
  • ✓ Evidence remains tied to its source
  • ✓ Documented damages show what is supported vs. missing
  • ✓ Deadline candidates require attorney confirmation
  • ✓ Readiness explains the blockers instead of hiding them in a score

Deterministic Where It Matters

OCR and language models may help propose extraction candidates, but TRACE does not depend on AI to run the Matter. Workflow, evidence provenance, authority rules, state changes, and human review remain deterministic.

Unknown stays unknown.

TRACE does not invent missing Matter IDs, dates, providers, plans, sources, or completion states.

Success means durable success.

A material action should not appear complete until the authoritative state or durable command exists.

AI failure does not stop the firm.

Manual claim, provider, records, damages, deadline, and readiness workflows remain available.

Attorney decisions remain human decisions.

TRACE surfaces facts, evidence, missing items, and review candidates. Legal judgment stays with the lawyer.

TRACE Is Not Your Practice Management System

Your PMS administers the firm. TRACE focuses on the pre-litigation work that determines whether an active Matter is complete, supported, and ready. No PMS replacement is required.

Keep in your PMS

  • General matters and contacts
  • Firm-wide tasks and projects
  • General calendar and email
  • Document management
  • Timekeeping, billing and trust accounting
  • Litigation docketing and discovery

Let TRACE handle

  • TRACE-generated Needs You / Waiting / At Risk items
  • Claim / coverage workflow state
  • Treatment, records, bills and evidence completeness
  • Documented damages development
  • Deadline candidates and attorney confirmation
  • Explainable pre-litigation readiness
The boundary: Your PMS administers the firm. TRACE develops and prepares the Matter. SETTLE uses verified Matter inputs for settlement intelligence and resolution support.
