What TRACE Owns

TRACE is narrower than a general PMS and deeper than a checklist. It owns the Matter-development state that determines whether the file is complete, supported, and ready for the next step.

Engagement

Representation review, conflict workflow, engagement package, consent/signature, and canonical Matter activation.

Claim & Coverage

Carrier, policy, insured, adjuster, claim number, representation notice, coverage/limits status, and follow-up.

Treatment

Providers, referrals, treatment episodes, expected follow-up, treatment gaps, and newly discovered provider review.

Records & Bills

Authorization lifecycle, request status, delivery, production, reconciliation, partial production, missing date ranges, and itemized bills.

Evidence

Source-linked facts tied to documents and pages, with review status, correction, dispute, exclusion, and chronology support.

Damages

Documented medical expenses, wage loss, property loss, out-of-pocket support, and evidence completeness — without turning TRACE into valuation.

Deadlines

Calculated deadline candidates, rule/source/version provenance, attorney review, and attorney-confirmed operational deadlines.

Client Requests

Focused requests for missing treatment facts, provider confirmation, wage support, expense documents, or evidence.

Explainable Readiness

What passed, what is unresolved, what is blocked, and why the Matter is or is not ready.

Deterministic Where It Matters

OCR and language models may help propose extraction candidates, but TRACE does not depend on AI to run the Matter.

Unknown stays unknown.

TRACE does not invent missing providers, dates, policies, bills, records, liens, losses, deadlines, sources, or completion states.

Success means durable success.

TRACE should not mark an action complete until the authoritative state, source, or durable command exists.

AI failure does not stop the firm.

Manual engagement, claim, provider, records, damages, deadline, client-request, and readiness workflows remain available.

Attorney decisions remain human decisions.

TRACE surfaces facts, exceptions, evidence, candidates, and blockers. The lawyer decides.

What TRACE Does Not Decide

TRACE is operational and evidentiary support. It does not replace the attorney, interpret medicine, or determine what a case is worth.

No legal advice
No medical advice
No liability conclusion
No pain-and-suffering multiplier
No settlement valuation
No negotiation recommendation

Product boundary: TRACE organizes facts, evidence, documented economic losses, deadline candidates, client requests, and readiness blockers. SETTLE owns settlement intelligence and resolution support. Attorney judgment remains with the firm.
