Frequently Asked Questions

Pricing & Billing
How does the pricing model work?
TrueVow has three public INTAKE plans: Solo ($499/mo), Growth ($1,299/mo), and Team ($1,999/mo). Every plan keeps all intake records visible and uses no public unlock fees or per-lead billing.

Solo includes 40 routed calls or 440 minutes. Growth includes 100 routed calls or 1,100 minutes. Team includes 200 routed calls or 2,200 minutes. Additional routed calls are billed at the plan overflow rate unless the firm upgrades.

All plans are confirmed through setup review, onboarding configuration, and a test call before activation.
Do you charge per lead or use unlock fees?
No. Public TrueVow INTAKE plans do not use per-lead unlock fees. Benjamin may flag priority intake opportunities based on configured criteria, but intake records remain visible inside the portal.

Your plan changes when your call volume, minute usage, attorney count, reporting needs, routing complexity, or support requirements grow.

Benjamin flags intake priority for attorney review. TrueVow does not determine legal merit.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Plan changes can be made through the customer portal after setup review. Start with Solo and move to Growth or Team as your call volume, reporting needs, or workflow complexity grows. Availability may depend on onboarding capacity.
Is Spanish really included?
Yes. Spanish intake support is included. During onboarding, the firm confirms when Spanish intake should be offered and how Spanish-language callers should be routed, booked, or escalated.
How It Works
Is Benjamin a chatbot?
No. Benjamin is not a free-form chatbot. Benjamin follows selected intake templates and onboarding settings, routing rules, escalation logic, and recording settings. It can support yes/no questions, structured responses, and approved open-ended prompts, but it is not designed to provide legal advice or make attorney judgment calls.
What happens when a call comes in?
For a routed call, Benjamin follows the firm's configured greeting, screening questions, escalation rules, booking rules, and recording/transcript settings. Benjamin creates a structured intake record and alerts the firm according to the plan and configuration.
What's the onboarding process?
1. Submit Firm Details — firm name, practice areas, and current intake setup
2. Confirm Call Setup — routing, practice areas, and booking flow
3. Test Benjamin — hear the intake path before activation
4. Activate Trial Coverage

Onboarding is limited to maintain quality and personal attention.
What practice areas does TrueVow support?
TrueVow is currently positioned for solo and small plaintiff-side PI firms. Additional practice areas may be reviewed during onboarding, but the public product is optimized around PI intake screening and attorney-review workflows.
Compliance & Security
What about compliance and data handling?
TrueVow is designed for controlled data handling, not zero-knowledge storage. Depending on firm configuration, the system may process contact details, structured intake answers, intake priority labels, booking details, timing or escalation flags, optional recordings, and optional transcripts.

Recording and transcript generation are optional and controlled by firm policy. Case analysis workflows do not require client identifiers where they are not needed. TrueVow is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice.
Are recordings and transcripts included?
Recording and transcript features are optional and configured during onboarding based on firm policy, disclosure requirements, and jurisdiction. Solo includes 7-day retention if enabled. Growth and Team include 30-day retention if enabled. Extended 90-day, 180-day, or 1-year retention options may be available inside the customer portal after setup review, depending on plan.
Who Is This For?
Do you serve large firms or enterprise?
TrueVow is built for solo and small PI firms. Larger firms or unusual workflows may be reviewed separately, but the public product is designed for small firm intake, booking, and lead-review workflows.
Why publish pricing instead of requiring a demo?
TrueVow is built for solo and small PI firms, not enterprise procurement committees. We publish starting prices so firm owners can understand the basic economics before they decide. Final activation happens after setup is confirmed inside the customer portal, because routing, recording policy, calendar configuration, and the intake workflow are confirmed first.
