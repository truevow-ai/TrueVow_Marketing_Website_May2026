Before You Compare Plans, Compare the Old Way.

Most firms do not feel the cost of intake all at once. It shows up in pieces: the missed call, the weekend catch-up, the staff interruption, the late callback, the paid lead that never became a record.

That old path may feel familiar. But familiar does not mean free.

Owner Time

The owner reviews voicemail, calls back, copies notes, checks email, updates case software, and catches up on weekends. That labor may not show up as a vendor bill, but it is still paid for with the owner's time, focus, and growth capacity.

Staff or Answering Coverage

A receptionist, intake assistant, or answering service may reduce missed calls, but often still leaves the firm with thin notes, callbacks, fragmented handoff, or a process that depends on someone else knowing what to ask. TrueVow is priced against the intake function — not just call answering.

Paid Opportunity at Risk

For a firm spending about $5,000/month on marketing, even a 38% after-the-ring gap can put roughly $1,900/month of paid opportunity at risk. The question is not whether intake costs money. The question is whether the current intake path is already costing more than it admits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Pricing & Billing
How does the pricing model work?
TrueVow has three public INTAKE plans: Solo at $499/month, Growth at $1,299/month, and Team at $1,999/month.

Plans are based on routed-call volume, included minutes, attorney count, reporting needs, workflow support, and support level. Every plan keeps intake records visible. There are no public per-lead unlock fees.

Solo includes 40 routed intake calls or 440 minutes/month. Growth includes 100 routed intake calls or 1,100 minutes/month. Team includes 200 routed intake calls or 2,200 minutes/month.

Additional routed-call usage is billed at the plan overflow rate unless the firm upgrades. Setup is confirmed before activation.
Do you charge per lead or use unlock fees?
No. Public TrueVow INTAKE plans do not use per-lead unlock fees.

Benjamin may flag priority intake opportunities based on configured criteria, but intake records remain visible inside the portal.

TrueVow does not determine legal merit. The firm reviews the record and decides what happens next.
Why publish pricing instead of requiring a demo first?
TrueVow is built for solo and small PI firms, not enterprise procurement committees. Firm owners should understand the basic economics before deciding whether to hear Benjamin live or review the 90-day trial path. Final activation happens only after setup is confirmed, because routing, practice areas, booking flow, recording policy, calendar configuration, and the selected intake workflow need to be reviewed first.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Plan changes can be handled through the customer portal after setup. Firms can start with Solo and move to Growth or Team as call volume, reporting needs, or workflow complexity grows.
How It Works & Setup
Do I need to change my case-management system?
No. TrueVow starts with the front door: routed calls, intake questions, booking, alerts, and structured intake records. No full case-management migration is required to begin.
Is Spanish included?
Yes. English and Spanish intake support are included. During setup, the firm confirms when Spanish intake should be offered and how Spanish-language callers should be routed, booked, or escalated.
Are recordings and transcripts included?
Recording and transcript features are optional and configured during setup based on firm policy, disclosure requirements, jurisdiction, and retention settings.
Is Benjamin a chatbot?
No. Benjamin is not a free-form legal-advice chatbot. Benjamin follows selected intake templates, routing rules, escalation logic, booking settings, and attorney-review boundaries. Benjamin does not provide legal advice, create an attorney-client relationship, run conflicts checks, accept or decline a case, or replace attorney review.
What happens when a call comes in?
For a routed call, Benjamin follows the firm's configured greeting, selected intake questions, escalation rules, booking rules, and recording/transcript settings. Benjamin creates a structured intake record and alerts the firm according to the plan and configuration.
What practice areas does TrueVow support?
TrueVow is currently positioned for solo and small plaintiff-side personal injury firms. Additional practice areas may be reviewed during setup, but the public product is optimized around PI intake, booking, priority alerts, and attorney-review workflows.
