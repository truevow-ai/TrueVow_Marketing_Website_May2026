What Benjamin Does During the Trial

Benjamin is not a generic receptionist and not a free-form legal chatbot. It is controlled intake automation built around your firm’s approved path.

Answers routed calls when enabled

Calls route based on your configured schedule, routing rules, and intake setup.

Qualifies the matter

Benjamin collects structured facts: incident type, timing, injury signals, treatment status, location, and urgency.

Grades the lead

Qualified leads are organized into grades (A+/A/B/C/D) so the firm can identify which opportunities deserve immediate attention.

Books or escalates

Benjamin can help route the next step: consultation booking, priority alert, follow-up queue, or structured review.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What happens after I get started?

    We review your firm details, practice area, and county availability. If everything looks good, we send you the onboarding preparation form before your onboarding call. Benjamin is activated only after setup, script review, calendar/routing configuration, and a successful test call.

    Is the trial free?

    No payment is collected on this page. Once your trial is activated, you can choose a paid INTAKE plan from the Customer Portal at any time. Your remaining trial continues normally and the paid plan begins automatically when your 12-intake or 90-day trial ends, whichever comes first. You do not need to choose a paid plan before starting the trial.

    Do I need to forward my calls?

    Yes. Benjamin handles routed calls based on your configured schedule, routing rules, and intake setup. You will need to configure call forwarding or provide phone routing access during onboarding.

    Is Benjamin a chatbot?

    No. Benjamin is not a free-form chatbot. Benjamin follows selected intake templates and onboarding settings, routing rules, escalation logic, and recording settings. It can support yes/no questions, structured responses, and approved open-ended prompts, but it is not designed to provide legal advice or make attorney judgment calls.

    What if my county is not available?

    County availability is confirmed during setup review. If your county is not yet supported, you may be placed on a waitlist and notified when availability opens.

    Is payment collected on this page?

    No. No payment is collected on this page. Once your trial is activated, you can choose a paid INTAKE plan from the Customer Portal at any time. If you choose a plan during your trial, your remaining trial continues normally and the paid plan begins automatically when your trial ends. You do not need to choose a paid plan before starting the trial.

    Can I buy directly from this page?

    No. TrueVow operates on a trial-first model. Start by telling us about your firm. After setup confirmation, onboarding, and test call, your 12-intake / 90-day trial begins. At any point during the trial, you can choose a paid INTAKE plan from the Customer Portal. Your trial continues unchanged and the paid plan activates automatically when the trial ends — no interruption.
