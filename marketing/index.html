Common Questions

What is TrueVow?

TrueVow is an intake architecture partner for solo and small personal injury firms. We help firms set up a controlled routed-call intake path with Benjamin as the voice layer, structured intake records, priority alerts, booked consultations, and attorney review workflows. TrueVow is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice.

What does Benjamin do?

Benjamin handles routed PI intake calls through the firm's selected intake path. It asks structured questions, captures key facts, flags priority opportunities, requests or books consultations based on configured rules, and creates intake records for attorney review. Benjamin does not provide legal advice, create an attorney-client relationship, run conflicts checks, or decide whether the firm accepts a matter.

Do I need to change my case-management system?

No. TrueVow starts with the front door: routed calls, intake questions, booking, alerts, and structured records. No full case-management migration is required to begin.

What does it cost?

INTAKE plans start at $499/month. Every plan keeps intake records visible. There are no public per-lead unlock fees. The 90-day INTAKE trial runs for 12 qualifying completed intakes or 90 days, whichever comes first. Setup is confirmed before activation. Full pricing is available on the Pricing page.

Is Spanish included?

Yes. English and Spanish intake are included. Callers can be routed through the selected intake path in the language they prefer.

Does Benjamin create a case?

No. Benjamin creates structured intake records for attorney review. The firm decides whether to accept or decline a matter, whether conflicts are cleared, and whether representation will be offered.
